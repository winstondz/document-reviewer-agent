# Acme Widget Pro — FAQ

## What is the battery life of the Widget Pro?

The Widget Pro has a battery life of approximately 18 hours of continuous
use on a full charge, or up to 3 weeks in standby mode. A full charge from
empty takes about 90 minutes using the included USB-C charger.

## Is the Widget Pro waterproof?

The Widget Pro is rated IP67, meaning it is dust-tight and can withstand
immersion in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes. It is not rated for
prolonged submersion, high-pressure water jets, or saltwater exposure.

## What is covered under warranty?

The Widget Pro ships with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover damage
from drops, water damage beyond the IP67 rating, unauthorized repairs, or
normal wear and tear such as battery capacity degradation over time.

## Can I use the Widget Pro with third-party accessories?

Yes, the Widget Pro uses a standard USB-C port and Qi wireless charging, so
most third-party cables and chargers are compatible. However, Acme only
guarantees full performance and warranty coverage when using Acme-branded
or Acme-certified accessories.

## How do I reset the Widget Pro to factory settings?

Hold the power button and the volume-down button simultaneously for 10
seconds until the Acme logo flashes three times. This will erase all user
data and settings, so back up your data first via the Acme companion app.
