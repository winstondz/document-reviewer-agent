Acme Corp — Employee Vacation Policy

All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per calendar year,
credited at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Part-time employees accrue
vacation on a pro-rated basis according to their scheduled hours.

Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two
weeks in advance for any leave longer than three consecutive days. Requests
for single-day absences may be submitted with 48 hours notice.

Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year, up to
a maximum of 5 days. Any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on January 1st
unless the employee has an approved carryover exception from their manager.

Employees who resign or are terminated will be paid out for any accrued,
unused vacation days as part of their final paycheck, in accordance with
state law.

New hires begin accruing vacation immediately upon their start date, but
may not use vacation days until they have completed 90 days of employment,
except in the case of pre-approved exceptions.
